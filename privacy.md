# Thermetery Boardview — Privacy Policy

*Effective: November 5, 2026*

Thermetery Technology LLC ("we") publishes Thermetery Boardview ("the app")
for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## We collect no data

The app does not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal
or usage information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no
tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

## No network access

The app does not connect to the internet. All parsing and rendering of board
files happens on your device.

## Files you open

When you open a boardview file, the app copies it into its own private
storage on your device so it can read it. These copies never leave your
device. iOS may delete them when it needs space, and they are removed when
you delete the app.

## Decryption keys

If you choose to remember a key for an encrypted format, it is saved as a
file in the app's private storage on your device. It is excluded from iCloud
and device backups, and you can clear it at any time from the app's key
manager. The app ships with no keys.

## Apple

If you have chosen to share crash reports and analytics with app developers
in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymized crash data.
That sharing is controlled by Apple and by you, not by the app.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Open source

The app's source code is available under LGPL-3.0-or-later at
<https://github.com/Thermetery-Technology-LLC/thermetery-boardview>, so
anyone can verify these statements.

## Changes

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and its
effective date before the change ships.
